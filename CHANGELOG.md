# Changelog

## 0.1.5

Current publication candidate, based on the installed 0.1.5 application sources.

- Hold Tab in Finder, hover a sector, release to convert.
- Six-sector wheel with an empty center, native foreground labels and per-sector progress.
- Immediate presentation while reading selection; cancelled or stale replies cannot reopen it.
- Immediate dismissal after saving and a 12-point menu bar icon.
- PNG, JPEG, WebP, available system AVIF, PDF, and ImageOptim codec integration.
- Original preservation, adjacent output, validation, cancellation, and handled-batch rollback.
- Portable codec discovery, MIT license and third-party notices.
- English and Russian instructions, real processing checks, and an isolated five-stage demo.
